PROBLEM STATEMENT

Streamline of Security Deposit / FDR Portal

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) requires institutional security deposits and FDRs as statutory guarantees. Manual tracking across hundreds of institutions creates critical operational vulnerabilities.

Manual Risk #1

Missing Instrument Records

Difficulty in maintaining institution-wise FDR documents, leading to missing certificates, fragmented physical registers, and misplaced deposit endorsements.

Manual Risk #2

Unmonitored Maturity Dates

Failure to identify instruments reaching maturity deadlines in advance, resulting in lapsed guarantees and delayed renewals without council authorization.

Manual Risk #3

Opaque Release Workflows

Difficulties tracking refund petitions, student clearances, and course closure NOCs, creating administrative delays and lack of centralized visibility.

Manual Risk #4

Delayed Discrepancy Detection

Absence of standardized scrutiny workbenches prevents timely detection of mismatch in lien markings, tenure durations, or deposit principal values.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Built Specifically for AICTE Regulatory Rigor

Replacing spreadsheet chaos with an enterprise-grade digital administration console.

📅

Automated Maturity Matrix

Live JavaScript date engine categorizes active, approaching (31–90d), expiring (1–30d), and overdue instruments with zero manual recalculations.

Document Scrutiny Interface

Side-by-side comparison distinguishes institution-submitted records from independently confirmed demo data, eliminating confirmation ambiguity.

Renewal & Replacement Linkage

Maintains explicit relational parent-child links between expired FDRs and replacement instruments with rollover amount and tenure tracking.
